%C A003961(k+1) - A003961(k) is always even and never divisible by 3, because A003961(m) is odd for all m and 3 divides A003961(m) iff m is even.
%C A heuristic singular-series model predicts that the number of terms <= N grows like c*log(N) with c approximately 0.55; the data up to 10^11 agree with this.
%C The list is complete up to 10^11 (search with a rigorous logarithmic filter followed by exact verification modulo two primes).

TASLAK 2 — Kaydırma aliquot fonksiyonu (önce OEIS'te "0,1,2,5,2,9,4,19,16,11" diye aratın; varsa bu taslak yerine ona yorum ekleyin)

%N a(n) = A003961(n) - n.
%S 0,1,2,5,2,9,4,19,16,11,2,33,4,19,20,65,2,57,4,43,34,17,6,111,24,25,98,
%T 71,2,75,6,211,32,23,42,189,4,31,46,149,2,123,4,73,130,41,6,357,72,97,
%U 44,101,6,321,36,241,58,35,2,255
%C For n >= 2, a(n) > 0 and a(n) has the opposite parity of n, since A003961(n) is odd and greater than n.
%C The map n -> a(n) has no fixed points (A003961(n) = 2n is impossible by parity) and no 2-cycles (A003961 is injective, so A003961(x) = A003961(y) = x + y forces x = y). Hence every cycle has even length >= 4.
%C Under iteration, every starting value n <= 10^7 eventually reaches 1 or one of two cycles: (12, 33, 32, 211) and a 12-cycle starting at 11500. About 10.8% of starting values n <= 10^7 enter the 4-cycle.
%C The average of log(a(n)/n) is about +0.88 over even n and about -2.47 over odd n, so trajectories shrink on average; conjecturally every trajectory is bounded.
%o (PARI) f(n) = my(f=factor(n)); prod(i=1, #f~, nextprime(f[i,1]+1)^f[i,2]);
%o a(n) = f(n) - n;
%Y Cf. A003961, A252748.
%K nonn
%A _Abdulkadir Özcan_, Nov 26 2026

YORUM ÖNERİSİ — A275717 (A003961(n) > A003961(n-1) olan n'ler)
============================================================
%C This sequence has asymptotic density 1/2. Sketch: F(n) = log(A003961(n)/n) is additive with convergent Erdős–Wintner series, so (F(n), F(n+1)) has a limiting distribution. The involution n -> -n-1 on residue classes swaps the roles of n and n+1, so the distribution of F(n+1) - F(n) is symmetric, and it is atomless by Lévy's purity law since sum over primes of 2/p diverges. Numerically the proportion is 0.49997-0.50005 for n near 10^4, 10^6 and 10^8. - _Abdulkadir Özcan_, Nov 26 2026
